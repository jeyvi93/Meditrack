# MediTrack

## Clinic Management System

MediTrack is a clinic management system developed as a capstone project for Mandaue City College. The system is designed to help manage clinic operations while providing students and patients with a convenient way to access clinic services.

The system is divided into two main sides:

- **Clinic Side** – used by clinic personnel to manage patient information, appointments, medicines, consultations, reports, and referrals.
- **Patient Side** – used by students/school staffs to book appointments, view appointment status, receive notifications, and manage their profile.

## System Modules

### 🏥 Clinic Side

The clinic side provides the main tools for managing the school's clinic operations.

#### Dashboard
Provides an overview of clinic activities and important information through summarized data and statistics.

#### Student Profiles
Allows clinic personnel to manage and view student information and patient records.

#### School Staff Profiles
Allows clinic personnel to manage information for teaching and non-teaching staff.

#### Appointments
Allows clinic personnel to manage and monitor patient appointments.

#### Medicine Inventory
Used to manage medicines available in the clinic, including inventory status and medicine-related information.

#### Consultations
Designed to record and manage patient consultation information, including complaints, vital signs, and other relevant consultation details.

#### Monthly Reports
Designed to provide summarized clinic records and statistics for a specific month.

#### Referrals
Designed to manage and generate patient referral records.

### 👤 Patient Side

The patient side provides students/patients with access to basic clinic services.

#### Book Appointment
Allows patients to request an appointment with the clinic.

#### View Appointments
Allows patients to view their appointment records and appointment status.

#### Notifications
Provides patients with notifications regarding their appointments and other clinic-related updates.

#### Profile
Allows patients to view and manage their personal information.

> The patient-side frontend is currently completed. Backend integration will be implemented after the clinic-side development is completed.
